Terms of Service — POS Assistant

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Scope

POS Assistant is private software with a single user: its author, who also owns the Google accounts it reads. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and accepts no other users.

Use

The software reads the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar, summarises what needs attention, and prepares draft replies in the owner's own account for the owner to review. It does not send, reply to, forward or delete email, and does not create, modify or delete calendar events.

Data

See the Privacy Policy. Message content stays on the owner's computer; only metadata is sent for classification.

The software is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. The author accepts no liability arising from its use.

Termination

Access may be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
